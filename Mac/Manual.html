Prerequisites

macOS 14 or later. Choose the download matching your Intel or Apple Silicon Mac. Move the app to Applications before opening it. An internet connection and your own ElevenLabs account are required.

Create a key on the ElevenLabs API keys page. Give it Models and Voices read access, plus access to the generation features you intend to use: Text to Speech for speech and dialogue, Speech to Speech for voice conversion, Speech to Text for transcription, Audio Isolation for noise removal, Forced Alignment for subtitle timing, and Dubbing write access for automatic translation. User read access is needed for the balance display. History and pronunciation-dictionary tools require their corresponding permissions; saving, adding or deleting voices requires voice write access. Use only the permissions you need. See the official API-key guide.

Available models, formats, voice slots and charges depend on your ElevenLabs subscription. Never share your API key or include it with a copy of this app.

First Run

  1. Open Settings with Command+,. On the API key tab, paste your key and choose Save API Key.
  2. On General, choose the default output folder. The default is ElevenLabs Speech Generator inside your Music folder.
  3. Use Test API Key to check Models, Voices and balance access without generating audio. Refresh Voices to load your choices.
  4. Choose a mode, voice and model; enter your text or choose an input file. Choose Generate and review the confirmation.

Speech and Dialogue

Speech tags: With Eleven v3 or v4, use Controls, Insert Speech Tag (Command+Shift+T). In the Dialogue editor, use Insert Speech Tag or the same shortcut. Search the examples or type your own instruction, then Insert places it before the cursor without replacing selected text. Save Custom Tag keeps an instruction for reuse; Remove Custom Tag removes your saved entry. The examples are suggestions, not an exhaustive catalogue, and their effect depends on the voice. Official Guide opens the current ElevenLabs prompting guide. Tags are delivery instructions, not paired opening and closing markup. The picker does not make an API request or spend credits.

Text to speech: Enter the words to be spoken, choose a voice and model, then Generate. The window title and character counter show the current limit. The counter conservatively counts UTF-16 units, so some characters such as emoji count as two.

Long speech: On General in Settings, enable Allow long speech text (split into parts) to enter up to 500,000 characters in Text to speech. This is off by default and does not extend Dialogue or other modes. The app splits within the selected model's request limit, preferring paragraphs, then sentences, then spaces between words. It never cuts a word or speech tag in half; an overlong unbroken passage must be shortened. The confirmation shows the number of parts and variations: every newly generated part is a separate paid request.

Long speech produces one WAV per variation at the selected sample rate. PCM parts retain their samples; MP3 parts are decoded into WAV without another lossy encoding step. Voice continuity across joins depends on the model. Previous-request context is used where supported; Eleven v3 does not support that context. Review the joins before publishing a long recording.

If a long request fails or is cancelled, completed parts remain under Details/Unfinished speech in the voice's output folder. Generate again with identical text, voice, model, format and settings to reuse verified parts and completed variations. Changed or missing retained audio stops recovery before another request is sent. Successful assembly removes its part files. A request accepted by ElevenLabs can still be charged if no completed part was returned; the app does not automatically retry such requests.

Dialogue: Choose Dialogue and Edit Dialogue. Add lines, assign a voice to each, and reorder them as needed. The complete dialogue can contain up to 2,000 characters and ten different voices. Choose OK to retain your changes; dismissing the editor without accepting discards them.

Voice Settings offers stability, similarity, speed, style and speaker boost where supported. Eleven v4 accepts stability and similarity; unavailable controls do not affect its requests. Models using discrete stability require 0, 50 or 100 percent. Language code is optional; leave it blank for automatic selection. Model availability is read from your account.

Transcription and Audio Processing

Transcription: Choose an audio or video file and a Scribe model. Identify Speakers enables speaker information in the details; Include Audio Events includes events such as laughter. An optional language code can guide recognition. Generate saves a text transcript and, when word timings are returned, SRT subtitles. Review automatic transcripts and subtitles before publishing them.

Voice changer: Choose an existing audio file, target voice and supported conversion model. Remove Background Noise optionally cleans the input. Generate creates a converted recording rather than reading text.

Voice isolation: Choose a recording to remove background noise without changing the speaker. Use at least five seconds of audio. The service supplies the audio format; the app preserves it with the correct MP3, WAV or FLAC extension.

Transcript alignment: Choose a recording and enter its existing transcript. Generate saves the text and SRT subtitles using the returned word timings. This matches known words to the audio; it does not recognise missing words or translate them. Review subtitle timing before publishing.

Uploads are limited to 500 MB per file. Selecting a file does not upload it; generation or an explicitly confirmed voice-cloning action does. Service limits may be lower for a particular request.

Automatic Dubbing

Open Automatic Dubbing from Tools (Command+Shift+B). Choose a local audio or video file, or a public HTTPS link, then the source and target languages. Automatic detection is available for the source language; you can also enter a supported language code. Give the job an optional name and choose Start Dubbing. Review the paid-request confirmation before proceeding.

The app saves the accepted project in the app's Application Support folder, inside Dubbing jobs. Progress appears in a separate readable status field. Cancel Request stops local monitoring, not processing or charging on ElevenLabs. Resume Job checks that same saved project without submitting another paid request. Open Job loads a different saved job; New Job prepares a separate request without deleting previous projects.

Completed lossless audio is saved as FLAC or WAV inside the output folder's Dubbing subfolder, grouped by target language. Existing recordings are preserved. If a submission fails before a project ID is returned, check your ElevenLabs dubbing projects before trying again: the service may already have accepted it. Dubbing preserves speaker identity where the service supports it; rights, consent and account restrictions still apply.

Voice Library, Design, Remix and Cloning

The Voice Group selector beside Voice filters all loaded account voices into Your Voices, Cloned, Designed, Default or Shared. Cloned includes instant and professional clones; Your Voices uses the ownership information returned by ElevenLabs. Changing the group does not change the current voice: if it falls outside the group it remains selectable and is labelled accordingly. All Voices means the voices available in your account, not the entire public library.

Preview Voice beside the voice picker plays an existing preview supplied by ElevenLabs, without generating speech or spending generation credits. It is unavailable when the selected voice has no preview. Command+Option+P starts the preview; Escape stops playback.

Open Voice Library from Tools. Search your own voices or switch to shared voices. Preview a voice before choosing it, and add a shared voice to your account when desired. Removing an account voice requires confirmation and may affect other projects that use it.

Voice design: Describe the voice in 20 to 1,000 characters. Generate returns audio previews. Listen to them and choose Add Preview Voice to save a selected preview to your ElevenLabs account under a name you choose.

Voice remix: Choose an existing voice and describe the changes in 5 to 1,000 characters. Generate returns previews without changing the original voice. In either design or remix, choose Add Preview Voice to save your selected preview as a new account voice after confirmation.

Clone voice: Open Clone Voice from Tools or press Command+Shift+N. Enter a name, optionally describe the voice, and add recordings. You must confirm that you have the necessary rights and consent before uploading them. Account eligibility and ElevenLabs verification requirements still apply.

During cloning, Clone Status shows upload progress and then the wait for ElevenLabs to create the voice. Inputs and Close are unavailable while uploading; Cancel Upload (Cancel Request on Mac) stops the local request. The final result stays in this readable field. If cancellation or a connection failure occurs after the service accepted the upload, check your Voice Library before creating the same voice again.

Pronunciation Dictionaries

Open Pronunciation Dictionaries from Tools. Select up to three dictionaries to use with speech or dialogue. You can create an alias or phoneme rule, import or export a PLS file, and add a rule to an existing dictionary. Alias rules replace written text with a chosen pronunciation; phoneme rules use IPA or CMU notation. Support varies by model. Refresh after editing a dictionary and select its latest version.

Files, Projects and Playback

Generated files are saved inside the default output folder in Settings. Speech and voice-conversion recordings are grouped in a subfolder named after the selected voice. Dialogue uses a Dialogue folder; voice-design previews use Voice design; remix previews use Voice remix; noise-removal recordings use Voice isolation. Transcripts and aligned subtitles remain in the main output folder. Existing files are not moved, and repeated generations never overwrite earlier recordings. For speech, dialogue and voice conversion, MP3 is the initial default; choose another default on the Audio tab. Saved projects retain their own format. PCM output is saved as a standard mono WAV file at the chosen sample rate. Some higher-quality formats require a higher subscription tier.

Enter a base filename, or leave it blank to use the beginning of your text. Spaces are preserved; characters unsafe in filenames are replaced. Multiple variations add _v1, _v2 and so on. If a filename already exists, a numbered suffix keeps the existing recording.

The generated-files list offers playback and Save Selected for another copy. Return in the list plays the selected audio; Escape stops playback. Audio Settings in Tools opens the Audio tab of Settings, where playback device, default output format and automatic playback are managed together. Generation History lists recordings held by ElevenLabs and allows playback or download. A voice-design preview can also be saved as an account voice.

Save Generation Details writes JSON inside the Details subfolder. Audio-generation details include the reusable speech project and request information; transcription details contain word timings and speaker data. Save Project stores your text, voice choices and settings as a .speech.json file. Open accepts a saved speech project or a plain-text file. Projects do not include the API key or embed input recordings; keep referenced recordings available when moving a project.

Mode drafts are saved automatically after a short pause in editing, and when switching modes or closing normally. Saving a project is recommended for work you want to keep independently. If a saved voice or model is unavailable, its selection is retained and labelled Unavailable rather than replaced silently. Cancellation keeps completed files and removes incomplete downloads. An accepted request can still be charged even when cancelled.

On the Audio tab of Settings, choose the playback device and default output format, and optionally enable Play new generations automatically in sequence. This is off by default. Only new audio from a successfully completed batch plays, one file at a time, including voice-design and remix previews. Completed dubbing also follows this preference. Transcripts are not played. Cancelled or failed batches do not start playback automatically. Stop ends the current audio and the whole queue. Manually playing a result replaces the queue; changing the playback device stops it.

Press F1 for brief help about the focused control. The help text is read-only and can be read a line at a time. Close or Escape returns to the same control; Open Manual opens the full guide. Dialogue editing is available only when Dialogue mode is selected and no request is active; it does not change modes.

Credits and API Key Testing

The separate Credit Balance field refreshes at launch and after generation. Command+B focuses it; Command+R refreshes it. It shows the included allowance remaining, usage and reset information reported by ElevenLabs. This is not a guarantee that the next request will be free or that overage billing is disabled.

Test API Key checks read access to Models, Voices and subscription information. Results appear in a readable, selectable dialog. A successful read test does not establish permission to generate, clone voices or modify dictionaries. If the balance fails while generation works, check User read permission.

Use ElevenLabs usage analytics to inspect account activity, and review your subscription and usage-based billing settings on the ElevenLabs website. The app does not change your billing preferences.

Updates

Update checks are enabled at startup for new installations and can be turned off in Settings. Check for Updates is in the application menu. An available update can be downloaded and verified before installation. Automatic silent installation is optional and disabled by default. Updates preserve your preferences, drafts and recordings and reopen the app after replacement. Installation is postponed while a request is active.

Privacy and Troubleshooting

Your text and selected recordings are sent to ElevenLabs when you confirm an operation. Recordings, transcripts, drafts and saved project files can contain personal material; protect them accordingly. The API key is stored in this user's macOS Keychain. Settings and drafts use the standard user preferences and Application Support locations.

Requests that may spend credits are not automatically retried after failure. A network timeout does not prove that ElevenLabs did not accept a request. Check account history before repeating an expensive operation. HTTP 401 can mean an invalid key or missing permission for the requested feature. Review the key's allowed access. Check the Status Log for details and keep a saved project before troubleshooting.
